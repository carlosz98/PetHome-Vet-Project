About PetHome

From adoption center to neighborhood clinic

PetHome started by matching rescue pets with families. So many of those families asked us to keep caring for their pets that we opened a full veterinary clinic.

How we work

What you can expect at every visit

Estimates before treatment

You see the cost and approve it before we start. No surprise line items at checkout.

Low-stress handling

Treats, non-slip tables, a cat-only exam room and no rushing. Calm pets get better exams.

Records you can see

Visit notes, vaccine dates and invoices go into your My Pets portal after every appointment.
