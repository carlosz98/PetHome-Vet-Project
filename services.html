Vaccine schedule

Which shots does my pet need?

Core vaccines protect every pet. Lifestyle vaccines depend on where your pet goes and who they meet.

  • Dogs, core: Rabies and DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus, parainfluenza).
  • Dogs, lifestyle: Bordetella for daycare and boarding, Leptospirosis for parks and puddles.
  • Cats, core: Rabies and FVRCP, even for indoor-only cats.
  • Cats, lifestyle: FeLV for kittens and cats who go outside.
